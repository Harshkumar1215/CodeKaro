About CODEKARO

CODEKARO is an educational practice platform created to help students practice MCQs based on the topics covered through the CODEKARO YouTube channel.

Students can watch the lecture on YouTube and then use CODEKARO to practice questions, check their answers, and improve their understanding of the topic.
